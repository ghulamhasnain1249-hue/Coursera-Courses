We are ready to get started with our first lesson in this course on Analytics
for Marketing. In this lesson, we will take a deep dive into segmentation, focusing on the mathematical
methods to find our audience and speak to them using the most relevant
message possible. This will take us
from the theoretical who to the data-driven who. In Lesson 2, we will talk
about k-means clustering, the most common statistical approach to developing segments. We will focus on how
we go from analysis to implementation and how
this ladders backup into our segmentation strategy. In Lesson 3, we will apply our learnings to
a company walking through how they can go from analysis to
segmentation strategy. By the end of this week, you'll be able to develop
segmentation strategies of your own and apply them
to your marketing work. Let's get started.
