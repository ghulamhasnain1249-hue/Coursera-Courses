they have in common. Segmentation leads to targeting in platforms like
Facebook and Google. In order to know who
we want to target, we need to perform a
segmentation analysis first. Segmentation can
come in two forms. The first is segmentation
developed from a persona. Your persona could
be derived from both quantitative and
qualitative insights. Once you have your persona, you will then need to parse it out into characteristics
that matter. Think to yourself what
elements make up this persona. For example, sometimes age may really matter in a persona, sometimes age doesn't
matter at all, and occupation is
much more important. The second form of segmentation is segmentation developed from data analysis. This can
come in the form of k-means clustering or other
statistical analysis. We will spend most
of our time here. This is a much more in-depth way of segmenting our audience. Why practice segmentation? Segmentation helps us reach the right users with our media. Targeting the whole world with ads would lead to waste
of media dollars. Instead, we want to find our correct audience and reach them with
the right message. Segmentation helps us
know our audience better. It also makes it easier
to set up campaigns in our audience buying
platforms like Facebook. If we know our
audience in advance, we can easily look for segments
that reach that audience. Now that we understand
