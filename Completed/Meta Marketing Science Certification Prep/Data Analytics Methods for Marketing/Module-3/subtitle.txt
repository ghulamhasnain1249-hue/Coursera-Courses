As a marketing analyst, one of the most common
questions you'll try to answer with data analysis is, did our advertising
campaign work? This may seem like an
obvious question and we can intuitively think of
different ways to answer it. However, getting
the answer right isn't as straightforward
as it may seem. This week we will walk through different methods you could use to help answer this question. First, in this lesson, we'll take a look at what advertising effectiveness really means and which
different approaches exist to measuring it. Then we'll dive deeper
into the use of experiments for advertising
effectiveness measurement. In the third lesson we'll
look at how you can optimize ads and make them
more effective with AB tests. By the end of this
week you'll have a few more tools in your
marketing analyst toolkit. Let's get started.
