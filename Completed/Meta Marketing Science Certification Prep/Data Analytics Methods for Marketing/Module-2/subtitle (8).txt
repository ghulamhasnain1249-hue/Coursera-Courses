how many customers a business has lost during a given time period. Churn rate is generally
calculated on a monthly or annual basis depending on sales volume. Churn rate is calculated

to finding churn rate. First, we have to subtract the most recent
number of customers from the previous in order to get a number
of lost customers. So in this case we subtract
702 from 791 to get 89. Second, we divide a number of
lost customers by the users at the beginning of the period,
so 89 divided by 791 to get 0.11. Lastly, since we wanted to be
a percentage we multiply by a 100 to get 11% churn rate. This is the churn rate
over the period of a year. For Calla & Ivy, this means that
11% of the customers they had in 2019 are no longer customers in 2020,
they have churned. Now how does churn rate fit into
the average lifetime of a customer? Going back to our average
lifetime formula, we then divide 1 by the churn rate,
in this case 1 divided by 11% to get 9. Therefore, Calla & Ivy's average
customer lifetime is about 9 months. To translate that to years we divide
9 by 12 to get 3/4 or 0.75 years. Great, we have the first
piece of data we need, next we need to calculate

the average number of transactions. Calla & Ivy returned to the 2020 data and
