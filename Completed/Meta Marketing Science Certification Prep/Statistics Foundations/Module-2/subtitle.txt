Welcome to week 2 of our
statistics foundations course. During the second week, you'll learn about making predictions with
inferential statistics. In the first lesson
of this week, you'll learn all about sampling. Sampling is an important
concept that will help you understand how
your data was acquired, and who it represents. First, you'll be introduced
to what a sample is, and why using one is a
standard statistical practice. Second, you'll learn how to identify appropriate
sample sizes, and the impact on results
when a sample is too small. Finally, you'll learn about different sampling techniques, and when to use them. Getting the sample
right is the first step in a solid statistical analysis. Even the most advanced
statistics can produce inaccurate results if sampling best practices
are not followed. Ready to learn all
about sampling? Let's get started.
