In this lesson, we're going
to explore distributions. Distributions are a fundamental
statistical concept that tells you how your
data is spread out. They can also help you
predict what behavior people or customers might take
regarding a specific decision. First, we'll describe how
to find a distribution. Then we'll explore some
common distributions that you can expect to
find as a data analyst, including normal uniform piss and exponential
distributions. After that, we'll examine data shapes and
finish this lesson by learning about Z-Scores and how to use them
to judge a value. This lesson will provide some basic concepts that
you will build on later.
