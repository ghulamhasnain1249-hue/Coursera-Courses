What you can accomplish with your
data largely depends on what type of data you have. In this lesson,
you will learn about variable types, how to identify them,
and how to apply them. Variables are measurements or
descriptions of a thing like an object, a person, or a place. It could be the customers who walk
into your store or visit your website, the traffic patterns at busy
intersections, the average temperature for major cities, or anything that can be
counted or measured in spreadsheets. Each column often represents
a different variable, such as age, return on investment,
location, and names. However, numerical variables like age and categorical ones like location
require different types of analysis. That's why understanding
variable types is crucial. In this lesson, you will learn about
variables that are numbers or quantitative variables, and what's considered
a discrete or continuous variable. Then you will learn about variables
that are groups or qualities known as qualitative variables, including how to
identify nominal and ordinal variables. After that, you will learn about
the difference between independent variables and dependent variables and
how to use them. This is a fair amount of information,
but it is important to understand what kind of data you have before you
can figure out what to do with it. I'm excited to get started.
