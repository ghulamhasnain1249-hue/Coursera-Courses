This lesson is all
about cluster analysis. If you recall from earlier videos, cluster analysis allows you to
break your data down into groups or subsets so that you can identify
audiences and populations more easily. Which will help you with
your marketing efforts. First, you'll learn about
the concepts behind cluster analysis, and the assumptions you have
to meet before you can run it. And then you will learn
how to perform a simple cluster analysis using
the Tableau software. Cluster analysis, is a useful tool that will
come in handy as you learn better ways to gain insights into your
target audience, as a marketing analyst.
