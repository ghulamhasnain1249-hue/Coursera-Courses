The last model you will learn
is a time series analysis. A time series analysis
charts the numbers over time and allows you to predict what they will
be in the future, like being able to forecast sales or return on investment. In this lesson, you will
cover time series analysis, including what they are and the assumptions you have to
meet in order to run them. Then you will learn
how to create this new model using Tableau. Time series analysis is a very useful tool that you will use regularly as a data analyst. Let's get started.
