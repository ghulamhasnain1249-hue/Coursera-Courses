In the last lesson, you got
an introduction to experimental design and hypothesis. In this lesson,
you'll learn about hypothesis testing and AB testing and how they're necessary
tools in any data analyst toolkit. First, you'll get an introduction
to hypothesis testing, an AB testing, and
the fundamental concepts behind them. Next, you'll learn about P-values,
what they are, and why they are important. After that, you'll learn what
confidence intervals are and how they can affect your conclusions. Finally, you'll learn how to actually
run a simple hypothesis test using a spreadsheet. These are concepts that you need to
understand in order to do your job as a data analyst on a practical level. A thorough understanding of these
concepts will help you interpret your results correctly and
take action on your findings.
