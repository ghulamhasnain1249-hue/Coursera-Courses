In any type of analytics, making a simple mistake that will
invalidate all of your results is easy. Most of the time, people don't even
know that a mistake was made, and important decisions are based
on bad information. In this lesson, you're going to be introduced to some
of the common mistakes in statistics. First, you will learn about bias,
what it is, how it can appear when giving a survey
outside of a familiar place or culture, how it can affect your results,
and how to avoid it. Then you'll learn about false
positives and negatives that may appear in your data, otherwise
known as Type I and Type II errors. Finally, youll learn about assumptions. You'll learn the four types of assumptions
typically made about an analysis and why it's important to ensure that
the assumptions the analysis makes about your data are true. This lesson is not as technical as some
others, but it is no less critical. Learning these lessons now, and
learning them well will save you a lot of wasted effort when working
with statistics as a data analyst.
