Like its companion, variance,
the standard deviation is another tool for understanding uncertainty, especially in
quickly changing markets like real estate. In this video,
we will explore the standard deviation, which is the last way to
measure the spread of data. The standard deviation measures
how much individual data points typically deviate from the mean in
the same units as the original data. Standard deviation is easier to
understand because it uses the same units as the original data. In contrast, variance measures the average
squared deviation from the mean. Let's go back to our neighborhood example. The standard deviation is measured in
the same units as the original data, such as dollars. For example,
if the standard deviation of neighborhood house prices is $50,000,
it means that, on average, individual home prices differ
from the mean by about $50,000. This means that on one side,
the mean home prices would be $150,000, which is found from 200,000 -50,000 and on the other side of the mean,
the home prices would be 250,000, found by taking 200,000 and
adding 50,000 to it. However, variance is measured in
squared units of the original data. For instance, if the variance of neighborhood
house prices is 2,500,000,000, it represents the average squared
deviation from the mean selling price. While this measure is useful for some
calculations like statistical analyses, it takes more work to
understand directly in dollars. For this reason, most people use
the standard deviation like our example of $50,000 as opposed to
the variance of 2,500,000,000, which seems astronomical and
out of place next to the prices of homes. Just like variance, standard deviation is
most often calculated using technology. You will learn more about that as we
explore using spreadsheets to find measures of spread. Lastly, the standard deviation has
the unique quality of telling us the rough percentage of the number
of homes between two values. If the distribution of home
prices is what we call normal, then 68% of homes will be
priced between 150,200 $50,000. This is great news if your
budget is between those values. The standard deviation is another way
to make informed decisions based on how spread out the data is to help
you make everyday decisions.
