Hi, and welcome to Statistics Foundations explicitly designed
for Data Analysts. This course is tailored
to equip you with the statistical tools necessary for insightful data analysis, moving you from making
assumptions about data to data driven strategies that
make a substantial impact. Statistics is a
pivotal tool that enables analysts to
decipher complex data, spot trends, and model
future outcomes effectively. This comprehensive course is structured into five main parts, each intended to be
completed over a week, culminating in a hands
on capstone project. In the first week, we'll explore descriptive statistics to
help you understand and summarize your data and provide quick insights to
answer preliminary questions. The second week introduces
inferential statistics, which allows you to
make predictions and generalizations about
a large dataset from a smaller sample. This is essential for
effectively scaling analysis and is foundational
in any analytics work. Week 3 focuses on experimental design and
hypothesis testing. You'll learn how to set up
and analyze experiments, interpret the results, and recognize common pitfalls
and data analysis. The fourth week is particularly exciting as we explore
data modeling. We'll cover various models, their applications
and how to choose the right model for
your data scenario. We'll conclude in
the final week with the Capstone project that integrates all of the
concepts you've learned. Throughout the course,
I will guide you through each concept
with step by step explanations and
relevant examples to ensure you learn and apply
these skills effectively, so let's get started.
