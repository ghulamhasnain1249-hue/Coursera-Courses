Variance plays a role in
rapidly changing markets like real estate and helps people who use statistics to
measure uncertainty. In this video, we will continue to explore the
prices of homes in a neighborhood to understand better how to find the
variance in prices. Variance is a number that
explains how spread out the data points are from the
datasets mean or average. The idea of spread out data might seem hard to
follow right now. Let's look at an example to help make sense of what
this definition means. Picture yourself searching for a home in a particular
neighborhood. Your budget is set
200,000 and 250,000. Ideally, you'd expect
the average home price to match this range. However, variance
comes into play, potentially affecting
how many homes fall within your budget due to fluctuations in
the average price. Let's explore this. In the case of low variance, home prices in the
neighborhood tend to group closely
around the average. For buyers, this implies that if the average selling prices
align with their budget, they can expect a
broader array of homes within their
200,000-$250,000 range. With prices remaining
steady near the average, buyers can anticipate more consistent and
predictable options that fit within their
budget constraints. If there's a high variance, it means home prices
differ largely from the average and
are more spread out. This suggests that even if average home prices
fit their budget, buyers might find
fewer options in their 200,000-$250,000
range because prices spread further from the average. With prices fluctuating
widely around the average, buyers may face more uncertainty and fewer choices
within their budget. In many professions, variance is calculated
using technology. Later, you will see how to use spreadsheets to
calculate variance. Now, let's look at
another calculation measuring the spread
out of data, the range. The range measures how
far your data is spread out between the highest
and lowest values. In our home buyer example, the range shows the gap between the highest and lowest priced
homes in the neighborhood. Knowing this can help
buyers negotiate and understand how their budget fits the market and therefore, help them make informed decisions
about their investment. A high range might
mean the potential for overpaying while a low range suggests stable prices
in the neighborhood. While variance is often
calculated using technology, the range is a much simpler
calculation that is often done by hand or
using a basic calculator. For example, if the
highest selling price of a home is 350,000, and the lowest is 150,000, the range would be $200,000. We know the range is 200,000
because we subtracted the lowest price of 150,000 from the highest
price of 350,000. If we were given a dataset
of values to find the range, then we would simply subtract the lowest number from
the highest number. For example, if the
prices for current for sale homes included: 240,000, 350,000, 185,000, and 130,000. Then we would find the range by subtracting the
lowest number, 130,000 from the highest number, 350,000 to get 220,000 as the range of prices
in the neighborhood. So whether you're shopping
for your dream home or comparing furniture
prices at different stores, the spread of data can help
you make informed decisions.
