Statistics is really all about
describing the world around us and making sense of it. Measures of spread allow us
to think about how varied or similar the data points in a set are,
giving us insights into the diversity or uniformity of the information
we're analyzing. In this lesson, we will learn some ways to measure
the spreadsheet spread of a data set. First, we will explore variance and
range and how they help to describe
the spread of our data set. Next, we will explore what
standard deviation is and how it provides insights
into how the data is spread. And well also review how we
can use spreadsheets to find the standard deviation. Theres no need to worry. These concepts are simpler than they seem,
so keep that in mind as we get started.
