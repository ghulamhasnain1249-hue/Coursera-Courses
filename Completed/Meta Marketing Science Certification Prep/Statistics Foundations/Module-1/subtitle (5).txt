Welcome to week 1 of our
Statistics Foundations course. During this first week, you will learn about
descriptive statistics. Descriptive statistics
provide insights into the data you've collected, helping to understand various
aspects more clearly. These foundational skills are applicable not just in
professional settings, but also in everyday situations. In the first lesson, you'll learn how to use
measures of central tendency, such as mean, median, and mode to find the
middle of a data set and how to choose
when to use them. This is an exciting course in which you will learn practical skills for statistical analysis and a clear and
concise understanding of the concepts behind them. Let's get started.
