Capstone projects are an important
part of this course. They allow you to bring everything you
learned together into a cohesive project, letting you practice these skills and
cement your understanding. At the end of every week,
there will be a reading named Capstone. These readings are important. You will be given a template that
you will save on your computer. Every week you will fill in answers
using the skills you learned that week. This will not only test your knowledge
in what youve learned each week, but give you hands on practice that you can
use in your future career as a marketer. Capstone Week 1 will cover
descriptive statistics and will test you on topics like measures
of central tendency and variance. Capstone Week 2 will ask you to
find the different distributions in a data set through
the creation of histograms. In Capstone Week three, you'll practice
writing a hypothesis and testing it. Finally, in Capstone week four,
you'll take a data set and run an appropriate model on it. At the end of the course,
you will have filled out your template and have a cohesive Capstone project. Not only will it demonstrate
what you've learned, but you can add the completed Capstone
project to your portfolio to demonstrate your knowledge and
efforts to current or future employers. You will need to complete all four
capstone pieces in the template to finish the project and complete the course,
so make sure you don't skip any. Also, since the platform will not save
it for you, be sure that you save the template somewhere you know and
you will be able to find it later. Let's get started on this week's lessons.
