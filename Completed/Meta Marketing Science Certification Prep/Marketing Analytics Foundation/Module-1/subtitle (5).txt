If you spend some time online, you've
probably noticed that quite a few of the ads you see are for products
you're genuinely interested in and some of these ads may
have prompted you to buy. Well, that's no coincidence. You likely saw
these ads because some underlying data and marketing analysis made the advertiser
understand that you might be interested in the product advertised. That's just one of the many ways in which
marketing analytics powers marketing. In fact, it's fair to say that all marketing
benefits from data and analytics. This week we'll learn all about how
marketing and analytics go hand in hand. In this first lesson we'll take a look at the
role of a marketer and a marketing analyst. We'll hear from a few marketers and
we'll learn what core tasks they focus on. In our second lesson, we'll look at the five
main uses of analytics and marketing, and in the final lesson we'll look at examples
of marketing analysis in the real world. At the end of this week, you'll have a good
understanding of what a marketer does and the essential role analytics plays in
a marketer's job. Let's get started!
