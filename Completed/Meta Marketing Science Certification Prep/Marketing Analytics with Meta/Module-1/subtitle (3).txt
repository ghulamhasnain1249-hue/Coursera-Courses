Given how marketers have an ever increasing amount of
data at their fingertips, which can help them make
their campaign successful, it's no surprise that marketing analysts
are in high demand. Knowing analytics or having
access to an analyst is a real plus for any
marketer and as a result, new marketing analyst
roles keep emerging. We will give you
more insights into what marketing
analysts do and how data analysis forms
an integral part of marketing throughout
this program. But let me give you
a sense of the types of roles you will see
marketing analysts take on. In many advertising agencies, you'll find a
marketing analytics or marketing science department. Marketing analysts will
support the teams that managed client accounts by
providing them with research, evaluate the effectiveness
of campaigns, advising them on where they
should place ads, and so on. They will form an essential
part of creating and managing the marketing strategy
for the brands the advertising agency
is responsible for. You'll find that the most successful
agencies are the ones that blend great creativity with
excellent data analysis. Large companies, of course, also have their internal
marketing teams. They may work together
with agencies to help them execute their
advertising campaigns, but these marketing teams
will often determine the overall strategy and will optimize how the marketing money for the company is spent. They rely heavily on marketing analysts to
help them forecast, plan, optimize, and report
their marketing performance. Marketing analysts will
typically hold key roles in the marketing team or in a separate data analysis team that works closely
with marketing. Then there are those companies
that focus exclusively on marketing analytics
and that provide services for marketers who
would like to measure, optimize, and report their
marketing performance. But that don't have
the luxury of being able to rely on an
internal team or they may want to use
specific methods for which specific expertise or
tools are required. They will then turn to these
marketing measurement firms. You can think of
these companies as marketing analytics
agencies for businesses. There are a variety of roles for marketing analysts in
these types of companies, you can take on a sales
role where you sell marketing measurement tools to other businesses, or you might work on analyzing data from clients and creating
reports for them or you may work as an
account manager helping clients to understand
the data and reports. In smaller companies,
you may find that the marketer is expected to
know marketing analytics. Even in companies that have a marketing analytics or
marketing science department, marketers benefit a lot from having marketing
analytic skills, as it will help them understand how to measure and
optimize their campaigns and what questions to ask from the analysts or the marketing analytics agency they work with. Marketers really
benefit from having marketing analytics
tools in their toolbox. No matter which one of these
roles appeals most to you, after going through
this program, you'll be well-prepared
for any of them. Throughout the different
courses you'll go through, you'll find career
content that can help you to prepare if you
apply for a new role.
