You now have a good handle on the fundamentals of
Facebook Ads Manager. In this lesson, we will
get practical and take a look at how campaigns
are planned and scheduled. Ads Manager is a
self-serve tool and anyone wants to advertise
on Facebook can use it. In this lesson, we'll
first take a look at the starting point for creating
any ad in Ads Manager, selecting a campaign objective. Then we'll look at how to determine how much
you should spend on your campaign and then setting your budget
in Ads Manager. You'll also need to decide on a bid strategy and the
audience for your campaign. The last step in your
planning involves selecting where your ads will appear and
optimizing their delivery. As you can see,
preparing a campaign in Ads Manager takes
quite a few steps. Ads Manager guides you through these steps and the process
is straightforward. After this lesson,
you will know what goes into the planning
of a campaign and it will help
you to understand the results of the
campaign later on.
