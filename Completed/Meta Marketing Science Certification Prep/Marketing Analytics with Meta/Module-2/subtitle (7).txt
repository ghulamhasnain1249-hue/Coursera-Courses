campaign optimization. In other words, you pick a time window for which you
believe conversions are a result of your campaign and the Facebook system can learn
from the conversions during that time window
to further improve the delivery of your
ads and show them to those people who are
most likely to take the action you would
like them to take. There's a limited set of time windows you
can choose from, for most conversion
campaigns you will find four options, one day click, seven day click, one day click and one day view, seven day click
and one day view. Here you see what your selection looks like in Ads Manager. What does this actually mean? You can choose to attribute
all the conversions like for instance sales you
see to your Facebook campaign if the conversion
happens one day after the person
clicked on your ad or you can choose to include
all sales that happened in a window of seven days after the person
clicked on your ad. You would choose that
option if you assume that people may take a little
longer to decide to buy, this is actually the default
option in Ads Manager. You could also decide that
people don't really need to click on your ad to
be persuaded to convert, you may feel that someone who's viewed your ad and then buys within a day is
equally influenced by your ad as
someone who clicked. In that case, you
would decide to use the one day click or view
attributions setting. Alternatively, you could decide that someone who clicks
and comes back to your site and converts within seven days or someone
who views and converts within one day converted
as a result of your ad in which case this is the attribution setting
you would choose. Marketers will make
a different choice here based on the type
