Skills you’ll need for the exam
Assess your data and hypothesize
There are two things you’ll need to assess before starting any analytics task: 

The goals of the analysis and what KPIs will be used to measure them

The data you have access to and its source

You can use the SMART goal template to help you with the first point. You’ll use your own judgement and the parameters of the project at hand to decide which data to use and from what sources for the second point. From here, you’ll come up with a hypothesis about a behavior or other element of your campaign.

Recommend measurement solutions and perform an analysis
In this step of the marketing process, you’ll ask yourself what methods you’ll use to confirm or reject your hypothesis from the previous step. There are several tools and techniques you can use to choose your measurement methods, which can include cross-channel reach reports, attribution or marketing mix modeling. 

Generate insights and make data-driven recommendations
In this final step, you’ll be asked to generate some insights based on the data you’ve collected and organized. This will usually look like a visualization of some kind. Then, you’ll make recommendations on campaigns and other marketing activities that are driven by that data.
