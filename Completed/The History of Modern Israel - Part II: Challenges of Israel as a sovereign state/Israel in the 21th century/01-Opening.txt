The last part of our course, will explore current issues
that characterize Israel today. We will focus on the economic
nature of a startup nation, with its benefits and deficiencies. We will discuss the growing social
inequality among different segments of the society and the fragmentation and
deterioration of Israeli politics. Looking at the examples such as economic
transformation, social protest, cultural diversity, political instability,
and the security challenges can help us to understand the central
problems that Israel faces in the present. While trying to maintain its Jewish and
Democratic nature, Israel copes with internal contradictions
and paradoxes as well as difficulties of being situated in an unstable and
non democratic Muslim Middle East.
