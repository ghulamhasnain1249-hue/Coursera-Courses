This segment of the course attempts
to explore the very elusive topic of Israel's changing identity. There is no doubt that the Israeli
society is very dynamic and fluid. This section will demonstrate these
dynamics by focusing on major identity transitions such as Americanization and
privatization, the transformation from an apparently unified identity to diverse
identities of a multicultural society. And the challenge that post-Zionism and religious Zionism pose
to the Zionist ideology. We will also focus on
the expanding role of the Holocaust memory on the identity of so
many Israelis.
