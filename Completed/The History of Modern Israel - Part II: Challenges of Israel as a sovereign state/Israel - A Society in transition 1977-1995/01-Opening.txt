In this lesson, we will discuss
the essential developments that transformed Israeli society at
the end of the 20th century. We will begin with the events that
led to the political turnover and look at its immediate consequence
as well as its long term result. Then, we will focus on fundamental changes
in the Israeli economy and their impact. The next section, will cover the Israeli-Palestinian
conflict in the 80s and 90s. The first Lebanon War, terrorist attack, Palestinian life under occupation,
the first Intifada and the peace process that led to
the Oslo Accord in the 90s. The lesson will end with the assassination of
Israeli Prime Minister Yizhak Rabin and its consequences.
