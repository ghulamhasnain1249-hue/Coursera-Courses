One of the events that took the country
by storm at the beginning of the 60s was the trial of Adolf Eichmann. He was a Nazi top official who
bore the large responsibility for the systematic murder of European Jewry. He was kidnapped from his hiding
place in Argentina by the Mossad, Israel's intelligence agency. And was brought to trial in Jerusalem for
his crimes against the Jewish people. When I stand before you to lead
the prosecution of Adolf Eichmann, declared the Israeli prosecutor
in his opening speech. I do not stand alone. With me here are six million accusers, their blood cries out but
their voice is stilled. Therefore will be I their spokesman. In their name I will unfold
this terrible indictment. Holocaust survivors gave
chilling testimonies. Which, for the first time, opened a public discussion on
the Holocaust in Israeli society. The trial went on for
eight months and finalized with the guilty verdict and
the death penalty for Eichmann. This was the first and only execution ever to take place in
the history of the state of Israel. The Eichmann trial sent an important
message regarding the obligation of the State of Israel to
protect Jews all over the world. And reinforce its historical
role as a State of the Jews, responsible for the fate of world Jewry.
