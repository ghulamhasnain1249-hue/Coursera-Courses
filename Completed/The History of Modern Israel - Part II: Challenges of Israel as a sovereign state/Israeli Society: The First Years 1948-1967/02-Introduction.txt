In the first two decades of its existence, the new state of Israel
faced many challenges. Israelis at this period face economic
difficulties, insecure borders, political challenges and social upheaval. Led by the Labor Party, the new state absorbed huge number
of Jewish immigrants who came primarily from Arab countries as well as
significant number of Holocaust survivors. These projects and other,
required collective effort and a spirit of commitment at the expense
of individual needs and aspirations. Thus, we can define Israeli society in
those years as a mobilized society.
