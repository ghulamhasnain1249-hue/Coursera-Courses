the process of deNasserization. Because under Nasser,

foremost to redefine nationalism. It's not Arabism first and
above everything, but Egypt first. And when you concentrate on
the particular territorial homeland, then comes the idea that local interests are more important

change and for a peace process. So this happened and
this was not a simple process, of course. In a situations where

the Israelis on the same level. So a diplomatic process started,
1974, the separation of forces. 1975, the Sinai II Agreement

resolve the conflict by violence. And then the last act,
