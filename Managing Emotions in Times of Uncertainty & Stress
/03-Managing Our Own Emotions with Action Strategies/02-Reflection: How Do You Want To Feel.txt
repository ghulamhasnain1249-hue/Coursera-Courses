Let’s begin our exploration of emotion regulation strategies with a brief reflection. 

Think about the months ahead. How would you like to feel? What energy would you like to bring to your work? Take a moment to think about or jot down the feelings you’d most like to experience at work. 

You may be wondering about how your hoped-for feelings compare with how your colleagues want to feel. We asked thousands of school staff across our nation how they want to feel. Their answers are reflected below. 

A wordcloud of how people say they want to feel, including words like excited, happy, safe, calm, joy, confident, supported
You may already be experiencing many of these feelings. If so, that’s great! In that case, this section and the next one will provide you with some ideas for how to maintain these feelings over time. 

If you are not yet feeling how you want to feel, you aren't alone. Our hope is that the research-based strategies in this course will help you get closer to experiencing feelings you want to have more of the time.
