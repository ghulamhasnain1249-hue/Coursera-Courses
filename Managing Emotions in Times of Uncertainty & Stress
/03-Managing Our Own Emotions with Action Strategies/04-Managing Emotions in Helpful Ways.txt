Managing emotions begins with asking ourselves the question: is this emotion I’m feeling helping me? 

Remember, a pleasant or unpleasant feeling is not good or bad; we have to consider its purpose. For example, moderate anxiety helps us remember to socially distance. Admiration when we witness a good deed can motivate us to do something similar. Other times, our feelings are not so easily managed, nor should they be. For instance, with feelings like grief and sorrow, sometimes all we can do is be with them and allow them to run their course.

Whether we decide to maintain, shift, or sit with an emotion, all require some amount of regulation. We all know this; we’ve been doing this our entire lives.

While there is an almost infinite list of possible emotion management strategies, some are more helpful than others.
