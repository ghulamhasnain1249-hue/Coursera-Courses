If you’re having trouble naming how you’re feeling, you are not alone! Most of us were not taught emotional literacy, and we don’t get much practice labeling our feelings in everyday life. We tend to express how we are feeling with just a few words, like "good," "fine," "busy," and "okay." 

Just like math and science have their own unique vocabularies, emotions have a language of their own. When we have more words to describe our feelings, we are better able to understand them and communicate them to others. Communicating our feelings precisely is a pathway to managing them effectively. 

For example, stress may be relieved through meditation, but someone legitimately overwhelmed with work might need a different strategy--perhaps delegating or making a priority list.
