How are you feeling today? Take a moment and try to answer honestly. It may be harder to answer than it appears, especially these days as we grapple with a global pandemic, economic and political divides, and the long-overdue reckoning with racism in our country. These issues evoke complicated emotions that can be hard to convey in words.

Sometimes our feelings are trickier to pinpoint. We sometimes hear ourselves say something out of character--or maybe just feel off--but can’t quite figure out the cause. It happens to us all. In this section, we’ll offer some ways to be more accurate at identifying your own emotions.  

Given all of our demands, it’s easy to place our own emotional needs last. Many of us just don’t make time to pause and check in with ourselves. This section was built to help you do just that--slow down and really think about how you are doing emotionally. 

Let’s return to the original question: How are you feeling, really? With everything else you have to tend to, you may be asking: why does it even matter?
