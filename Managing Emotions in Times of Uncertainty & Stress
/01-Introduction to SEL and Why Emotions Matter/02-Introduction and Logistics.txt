Again, we welcome you to “Managing Emotions in Times of Uncertainty & Stress"!

This course distills the best advice from decades of research on emotions, resilience, and human development, in addition to the knowledge we’ve gained from hands-on work with over 2000 schools around the nation. It was developed by educators, teachers, clinicians, scientists, and psychologists at the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence, including Marc Brackett, Diana Divecha, Nikki Elbertson, Jessica Hoffmann, Susan Riello, and Robin Stern, with support from Sara Epperson and the Yale Poorvu Center for Teaching and Learning. 

In this introductory module, you’ll learn about the important role that emotions play in your daily life, as well as in the lives of the students in your school. You’ll explore the impact of emotions on attention and learning, decision making, mental and physical health, and performance. 
But first, let's cover some logistics about this course. 

Course Learning Objectives

This course is designed to:

Educate school staff in the science and impact of stress

Provide school staff with the opportunity to build and apply social and emotional skills and strategies

Support school staff in identifying and managing their own emotions

Offer strategies for helping students to identify and manage their emotions

Provide resources and ideas that are relevant during this time of heightened stress and uncertainty

Course Navigation

Coursera organizes content into "weeks." We don't expect each "week's" content to take you a full week to complete--in fact, it may only take you an hour or two! You can consider each week as a "section" as you proceed through the course.  

To navigate throughout the sections of the course, please click the "Mark as completed" button in the bottom right corner as you complete each item. This will prompt you to go to the next section. You can also click the “Next” button in the top right corner. 
 

Course Content and Organization

The first half of the course is designed to help you enhance your own emotional awareness, language, and regulation skills, while the second will focus on helping others--especially your students--to understand and regulate their emotions. At the end of each section throughout the course, you will accumulate your own personalized list of actionable strategies in the "Action Planning Pitstop" document. 

Throughout the course, you will see videos designed to further your knowledge of each concept. There are different narrators from the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence that you will hear in the videos, including Dr. Marc Brackett, the Center's director.
